# 🛡️ SafeChat

### Privacy-Preserving AI for Child Online Safety

SafeChat is an AI-powered child online safety prototype designed to identify potentially harmful online behavioral patterns and provide parents with actionable safety alerts while protecting private conversations.

> **"SafeChat doesn't expose private conversations. It turns safety-relevant patterns into actionable safety signals."**

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Children may face online risks such as:

- Cyberbullying
- Harassment
- Threats and intimidation
- Suspicious contacts
- Grooming indicators
- Other potentially harmful online interactions

Parents need awareness of possible safety risks, but exposing every private conversation can affect privacy and trust.

SafeChat aims to provide a balance between **online safety and privacy**.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

SafeChat detects potential safety patterns and provides:

- ⚠️ Risk classification
- 🧠 AI safety detection
- 📊 Safety scores and insights
- 🔔 Real-time safety alerts
- 📜 AI detection history
- 💬 Parent guidance
- 🔒 Privacy protection

The parent receives safety-relevant information instead of raw private conversations.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔐 Authentication
- Login
- Create account
- Forgot password
- Reset password
- Demo authentication

### 🏠 Family Overview
- Overall family safety score
- Current risk level
- Child safety cards
- Active alerts
- Recent safety activity
- Safety trends

### 🧠 AI Safety Detection
SafeChat can demonstrate detection of:

- Cyberbullying
- Threats / Intimidation
- Harassment
- Suspicious contacts
- Grooming indicators
- Self-harm indicators
- Safe interactions

Each result can show:

- Risk level: Low / Medium / High
- Demo confidence score
- Detected behavioral signals
- Timestamp
- Recommended parent action

### 📱 Device Activity Simulator
The prototype includes a controlled simulator for demonstrating different safety scenarios:

- Safe Activity
- Cyberbullying Pattern
- Threat Pattern
- Harassment
- Suspicious Contact
- Grooming Pattern

When a scenario is simulated, the application updates the safety status, alerts, activity history, and parent guidance.

### 🔔 Safety Alerts
- High, Medium, and Low risk alerts
- Alert details
- Read/review status
- Recommended actions

### 📜 AI Detection History
View previously simulated safety events with:

- Category
- Risk level
- Demo confidence
- Behavioral signals
- Timestamp
- Recommended action

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Guidance
SafeChat provides simple recommendations based on the detected risk level.

### 🔒 Privacy Center
SafeChat follows a privacy-first approach:

- Private conversations are not displayed on the parent dashboard
- Raw chat content is protected
- Parents receive safety signals and guidance
- The prototype does not perform covert monitoring
